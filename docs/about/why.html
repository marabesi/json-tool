Why json-tool? ​

Are you tired of accessing free websites just to format your JSON content? Me too. Besides the data protection issue, sometimes we just want to have it formatted and move on.

json-tool is a simple tool that does not track you, does not store your data and does not sell your data. It helps you format, validate and beautify your JSON strings — nothing more, nothing less.

According to ThoughtWorks in the tech radar 27 (item 76 under Tools), developers should hold the practice of formatting or sharing information in formatting tools that comply with data jurisdiction. json-tool is built with that in mind: all the processing happens locally, in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

Five years of open source ​

Five years ago, the project started out of necessity: a JSON formatting tool that could be trusted with sensitive data — one that would not send information to third-party servers filled with ads and tracking scripts. What began as a personal weekend project has quietly grown into something used by thousands of active users.
