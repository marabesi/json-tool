Understand the structure of a document at a glance, with statistics for the whole document and every nested shape.

Why json-tool? ​

json-tool is a simple tool that does not track you, does not store your data and does not sell your data. All the JSON processing happens in your browser, in a Web Worker, so the interface stays responsive even with large documents.
